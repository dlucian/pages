Lisbon Friday Drinks

Every Friday, 17:00 to 19:00, somewhere good in Lisbon — a park, a kiosk, a miradouro. The Thursday poster names the spot. Free, open, bring a cool friend. This page is the whole Instagram playbook: one loop, repeated weekly, in about twenty minutes.

Goal

Give the tradition a public home, so weekly promotion leaves the two group chats — and friends bring friends. Deliberately cozy: there's a lever for scale (see growth), and it stays unpulled.

01

Where it happens

The rotation

A different spot each week

Parks, kiosks, miradouros — Praça das Flores is home base, not the fixture. The Thursday poster names the week's spot; the Friday 17:00 story pins the exact point so newcomers can find the group.

Rain

Relocate, don't cancel

Wet Friday → Mercado de Campo de Ourique, Time Out Market, or 8 Marvila. Call it in the Friday 12:00 story at the latest.

Geotag

Tag the real spot, every time

The location tag on every post and story is the week's actual venue. Bio says: "this week's spot is on the latest poster."

03

What goes where

Feed

The mosaic grid

Two posts a week — Thursday poster, weekend photo — alternating into a mosaic. Keep one poster template so the grid has rhythm. The pinned intro card states the time, "spot announced every Thursday", drinks are BYO or from a nearby kiosk, bring a cool friend.

Film drops

35mm, weeks late, on purpose

Lucian shoots analog; scans arrive weeks later. Post them as an occasional carousel ("35mm · [venue] · [month]"). The delay is a feature — it proves the tradition has depth, and no other local account has the look.

Stories

Logistics and live atmosphere

Reminders, countdowns, golden hour at the spot. Ephemeral lowers the effort bar and feed permanence — not the consent bar. The "Fridays" highlight is permanent, so only consent-cleared frames land there.

Reels · confirmed 2026-08-28

One a month, only if it's free

If a good ten-second sunset-crowd clip already exists: pick, crop, post with the location tag, under five minutes. No produced edit, no trending-audio chase. See the decision below for why.

04

Photos without the awkwardness

Attendees are private people at a casual drink. The rule set keeps faces safe without a single vibe-killing conversation.

Default

Faceless by design

Backs, hands, glasses, wide shots from behind, the spot itself. No consent needed, and it photographs better than posed groups anyway.

Once per event

The blanket heads-up

Said casually, once:

"I take a couple of pics for the Instagram — wave me off if you'd rather stay out."
Faces

Ask in the moment

One sentence, and @mention whoever opts in — the mention is also what powers the one-tap repost. A photo tag doesn't do that.

"Posting a story for the account — you in or out?"
Group shots

Opting out is a joke, not a form

Announce it loudly, make hiding the fun part, and check the frame before posting. Codex calls this opt-out rather than opt-in — open question below.

"Group photo for the Instagram! Duck or hide your face if you're skipping work!"
05

How it grows from zero

The tag-to-repost loop

Friends don't post their own stories — so don't ask them to. A story @mention DMs each opted-in attendee the story with a one-tap "Add to your story" button. Every repost reaches a few hundred warm Lisbon locals. This is 80% of the growth, and it costs the attendee a single tap.

Hyper-local geotag, every time

Location-tag the week's actual venue on every post and story. Hashtags on feed posts only, always with the archive tag: #lisbonfridaydrinks plus the venue and neighborhood tags — never broad #lisbon spam. Bio points at the latest poster for this week's spot.

One DM a week to a Lisbon community account

Expat pages, event aggregators, neighborhood accounts: "free weekly open drinks, every Friday, feel free to share." One acceptance from a 10k-follower local page outweighs a year of hashtags. Also tag the kiosk that genuinely appears in a shot — local businesses reshare local content.

The bridge month

Keep posting in the two chat groups for about four more weeks — "we're on IG now, follow @lisbonfridaydrinks and share this week's poster" — plus one direct, personal ask to each regular. The account announcement landed in both groups 2026-08-28; 15 followers on day one.

06

Deliberately not doing

✕

Weekly reels, editing, trending audio. Reels reward volume and production; the return per minute at a 2–8 person meetup is terrible.

✕

Follow-for-follow, pods, bought followers. Inflated numbers with zero attendance value — it poisons the local signal.

✕

Polls, Q&As, engagement content. With no audience there is nobody to engage. The event earns the audience first.

✕

Brand voice. No "link in bio to register", no formal event copy. The register is: "I'm drinking a beer at this fountain, come stand near me."

07

The one contested call: reels

The council split exactly once. Both positions, and what settles it, side by side.

Chosen · confirmed by Lucian 2026-08-28

Skip the reels grind; ride reposts

At this scale, warm reposts from real attendees beat cold algorithmic reach. The effort budget stays intact, and the routine survives every busy week. Exception: one zero-effort reel a month.

Rejected · Gemini's position

Weekly 5-second reels as the discovery engine

True that only reels reach non-followers — but weekly production breaks the effort budget, and a local recurring event converts through friends-of-friends, not strangers scrolling.

Review trigger · week of 2026-10-22

If eight events produce fewer than ~4 attendee reposts total, first rule out the mundane causes (private accounts, an unclear ask, mention settings). Only then treat it as an event-shareability problem — and the counter-move is physical, not digital: a small flag at the spot that makes strangers walk over. Instagram then documents the growth instead of driving it.

Open · group shots

Keep duck-or-hide, or go opt-in?

Codex flags the game as opt-out consent. Alternative: "step into the frame if you want in" — safer, slightly less fun. Either way the frame gets checked before posting.

Resolved · rain policy

Relocation, not cancellation

Settled 2026-08-28: a wet Friday moves indoors — Mercado de Campo de Ourique, Time Out Market, or 8 Marvila — announced in the Friday 12:00 story.
